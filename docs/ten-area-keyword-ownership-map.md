## 1. Principles of Keyword Ownership

To prevent keyword cannibalization and maintain compliance with Google Search Essentials (avoiding duplicate and doorway page patterns):

1. **Zone Hub vs. Area Locality Page**:
   - The **Zone Hub** (e.g. `/areas/maha-chai`, `/areas/krathumbaen-omnoi`) serves as a commercial directory and regional logistics hub. It owns the broad multi-locality queries and acts as an index/gateway to its constituent areas.
   - The **Area Page** (e.g. `/areas/samutsakhon/maha-chai`, `/areas/krathumbaen-omnoi/krathum-baen`) owns the specific, high-intent transactional search query (`รถรับจ้าง{AREA}`, `ขนของ{AREA}`, `ย้ายบ้าน{AREA}`).
2. **Inter-Provincial Route Queries**:
   - Long-distance destination queries (such as `รถรับจ้างมหาชัยไปกรุงเทพ` or `รถรับจ้างหนองแขมไปสมุทรสาคร`) are linked directly to approved route corridors (e.g. `/route/samutsakhon/bangkok`) or regional service hubs rather than creating conflicting duplicate pages.
3. **Evidence-Backed Service Claims**:
   - Agriculture and garden produce transport is explicitly ambient-temperature cargo (no false cold-chain claims).
   - Bulky furniture and appliance transport is performed by standard enclosed box trucks with lifting crews (no unsupported heavy machinery crane claims).
